Privacy Policy

NPS Photo Collector

Effective date: September 26, 2026  ·  replaces the version of September 10, 2026

NPS Photo Collector ("the App") is a field-data collection tool published by HGS Engineering for documenting site observations on National Park Service lands. This policy describes what information the App handles and how.

What the App collects

The App captures the following on your device when you use it:

  • Photos you take with the in-app camera or choose from your photo library.
  • GPS coordinates when you tap a GPS button, with a photo when the entry has no position yet, and when you create a Location of Concern site. The App uses them on your device to suggest the park and the nearest location.
  • Compass heading, only while you have the compass turned on in the Sites list, to suggest which way a site photo faces.
  • Text you enter, including descriptions, location names, park and sheet selections, priorities, notes, tank-check notes and corrections, and site details.
  • Names of people you record for the audit report: the audit team (name and organization), the NPS environmental coordinator (name and title), and park personnel contacted (name and title).
  • Files you import — a park's SPCC tank tables, the previous audit's findings spreadsheet, a location list, or an App backup — which are read on your device.
  • If you use live backup or Send to SharePoint (below): your Microsoft work account's email address, to show who is signed in, and a random device name the App makes up (such as ipad-k3p9x) to label your files. The device name is not derived from your device's hardware.

Where your data is stored

Everything above is stored locally on your device, in the App's private storage (app container files, localStorage / IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on live backup or use Send to SharePoint. The App has no accounts of its own, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

When data leaves your device

  • Exports and backups you make. The Export ZIP, Word Report and Backup buttons produce files that go wherever you direct them (Files app, email attachment, AirDrop, etc.). The App itself does not transmit these.
  • Live backup (optional, off until you turn it on). After you sign in with your Microsoft work account and turn it on in Settings, the App sends — automatically, whenever it is open and online — a copy of your entries, sites, tank check and the names listed above; each photo; and a list describing the photos. They go to HGS Engineering's SharePoint (Microsoft 365), through HGS Engineering's server functions on Microsoft Azure. Uploads continue until you turn live backup off or sign out.
  • Send to SharePoint. When you tap it in the Export dialog, the export ZIP is uploaded directly to HGS Engineering's SharePoint.

Live backup and Send to SharePoint are features of the web version of the App.

Signing in with Microsoft

Signing in happens on Microsoft's own pages; the App never sees your password. The App receives your account's email address, and the hosting service keeps a sign-in cookie so you stay signed in. Only HGS Engineering accounts may upload. You can sign out at any time in Settings.

How data is protected

  • Everything sent from the App travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Your device holds no Microsoft passwords, keys or other credentials for SharePoint; HGS Engineering's server functions do the writing.
  • The server checks that you are signed in with an HGS Engineering account before accepting anything, and writes only within the audit folders.
  • An upload link for an export ZIP works for that one file only and expires on its own.

Permissions the App requests

  • Camera — to take photos directly inside the App.
  • Photo library — to attach existing photos to an entry.
  • Location (GPS) — to tag photos, entries and sites with coordinates.
  • Motion and orientation — only if you turn on the compass, to suggest which way a site photo faces.

You may decline any permission; affected features will be disabled but the rest of the App continues to work.

Third parties

The App does not sell or share your data, and it contains no advertising, analytics, or social-media SDKs. Microsoft provides the services the App runs on, acting for HGS Engineering: Microsoft Azure hosts the App and HGS Engineering's server functions, Microsoft Entra ID handles sign-in, and Microsoft 365 (SharePoint) stores what live backup and Send to SharePoint upload. Microsoft may log connection-level information (IP address, request time) per its standard hosting practice, and HGS Engineering's server functions record each upload's file name and the account that sent it in their operational logs.

The App's code libraries (JSZip, ExcelJS and docx) are downloaded from public content-delivery networks (cdnjs and jsDelivr) when the App first loads, and are then kept on your device. Like any website, those services see your IP address; none of your content is sent to them.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it. Copies in SharePoint are HGS Engineering project records, kept and deleted under HGS Engineering's records practices: turning off live backup or signing out stops further uploads but does not remove what is already there.

Children's data

The App is intended for National Park Service personnel and contractors. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your control over your data

  • Delete data on your device at any time by deleting entries in the App, clearing the App's storage in your device settings, or removing the App.
  • Turn live backup off, or sign out, in Settings.
  • To have files removed from SharePoint, contact HGS Engineering.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated effective date. The September 26, 2026 version adds the optional live backup, Send to SharePoint and Microsoft sign-in, and lists the names, compass heading and imported files the App handles.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to HGS Engineering. The project's source repository is at github.com/whittw1/NPS-Photo-Tool.
